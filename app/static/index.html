How it works

Take it apart.

Five parts. One number you can prove.

  1. 01 · ReadGemini reads every page.Photos, PDFs and handwriting in Hindi and English, each figure with its quote and page.
  2. 02 · CheckAgents audit like a verifier.Scope, missing months, the steel-in, bolts-out balance and which fuel went where.
  3. 03 · CalculateExact code, not the model.Em = AD × NCV × EF × OxF. The EU method, the same answer every time.
  4. 04 · TraceEvery figure threads back.Tap any number and the source document opens at the exact words Gemini read.
  5. 05 · ProveReady for the verifier.An Excel data pack shaped like the EU template, and a PDF evidence pack.

Paper in. Proof out.

Ten realistic documents from a fastener exporter in Ludhiana. Watch the agents work, open the evidence, then send the mill’s reply.

  1. The demo factory, Verkam Fasteners Pvt. Ltd., and all its documents are fictional. Each quarterly CBAM certificate price applies to goods imported into the EU in that quarter; certificates for 2026 imports are bought from February 2027.
  2. Gross cost = tonnes exported × embedded emissions × certificate price. Payable is an estimate after the free-allocation adjustment: 97.5% (the 2026 CBAM factor) × the default benchmark of 1.364 tCO2e/t for fasteners (IR (EU) 2025/2620). With verified actual data the producer calculates its own adjustment, so the real figure can differ slightly. The adjustment applies in both cases, so the saving does not change.
  3. Sources: Regulation (EU) 2023/956 as amended by 2025/2083; Implementing Regulations (EU) 2025/2547, 2025/2620, 2025/2621 and 2026/1740; European Commission quarterly CBAM certificate prices; IPCC 2006 Guidelines. CarbonTrail prepares verification-ready data; it does not replace the accredited verifier or the EU declarant.
  4. Your documents. Uploads are used only to run your analysis. They are stored in a private Cloud Storage bucket and deleted automatically within two days (a one-day storage lifecycle rule); only the summary figures and file names are kept. This is a hackathon demo, so please don’t upload documents you are not allowed to share.

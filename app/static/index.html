Paper in. Proof out.

CarbonTrail reads a steel manufacturer's bills, invoices and handwritten registers, applies the EU's CBAM method, and hands back emissions data that an accredited verifier can trace to the source page.

The demo factory is a fictional fastener exporter in Ludhiana with ten real-looking documents for April to June 2026: photos, PDFs and a register written in Hindi and English.

Since 1 January 2026
EU importers pay for the carbon in steel, aluminium, cement and fertiliser.
No data, higher bill
Without the exporter's own figures, buyers must use EU default values plus a 10% mark-up, rising to 30% in 2028.
Built on Google Cloud
Gemini reads the documents, Google ADK agents check and explain, and every number comes from exact code.

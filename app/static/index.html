01 The border has a carbon price

Every bolt shipped to Europe now carries its carbon.

Since 1 January 2026, EU importers of steel pay for the emissions embedded in what they buy. The exporter’s own figure counts only if it follows the EU method and is verified. Otherwise the buyer pays on EU defaults, plus a penalty.

€82.32per tonne CO2e, CBAM certificate price, 2026-Q3
+10% → 30%mark-up on EU default values, 2026 to 2028

Phone photos of LPG invoices. A mill’s statement of supplies. A production register written by hand in Hindi and English, with corrections in red ink. Small exporters have the data. They don’t have a carbon team.

03 Agents read. Code calculates.

Gemini reads every page. Agents check it like a verifier.

  1. ReadGemini extracts every quantity in parallel, with the exact quote and page.
  2. CheckScope, missing months, material balance, fuel use and shaky handwriting.
  3. CalculateThe EU formulas run in exact code. The model never does arithmetic.
  4. ProveEvery figure links back to its source, ready for the verifier.

04 Proof out

One verifiable number, and what it is worth.

5.36tCO2e per tonne of fasteners

On our demo factory, actual data beats the EU default. When the mill sends its verified figure, CarbonTrail recalculates: 5.36 → 3.25, and the buyers’ exposure falls by €37,948 in a single quarter.

05 Traceable by design

Every number opens the page it came from.

Each step shows the formula, the working and an evidence chip. Click it and the photographed invoice opens beside the exact words Gemini read. Diesel burned in the generator is excluded automatically, because for steel only direct emissions count.

  • Formula, working and rule on every step
  • Low-confidence handwriting flagged for a human
  • Excel data pack in the shape of the EU template

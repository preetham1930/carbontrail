CBAM is live.

EU importers of steel, aluminium, cement, fertiliser, hydrogen and electricity owe for the carbon embedded in what they import. That’s CBAM, the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism.

Straight answers

Good questions. No fine-print answers.

What a factory owner needs to know before the first upload.

Do I need clean spreadsheets to start?

No. Start with phone photos or PDFs of bills, invoices and registers. CarbonTrail reads skewed pages, handwriting and mixed-language records, including Hindi and English.

What if a number is unclear or a month is missing?

It is flagged for review, not quietly treated as reliable. You can check the source quote, add the missing bill or confirm a correction before relying on the result.

Can my EU buyer use the result straight away?

Your own figure counts only if it follows the EU method and an accredited verifier checks it. CarbonTrail gives you the data pack and the evidence trail for that check; it does not replace it.

Does AI calculate my emissions?

No. AI reads the documents and checks scope and quality. Exact code applies the EU formula, with every input and step linked to the original page.

Will my own figure always cost less than the default?

Not always. CarbonTrail shows the comparison in euros so you can see the difference. The figures on this page come from a fictional demo factory, not a promise of savings.

What happens to my documents?

They are used only to run your analysis, kept in a private Cloud Storage bucket and deleted automatically within two days. Only the summary figures and file names are kept.
